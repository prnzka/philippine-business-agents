# Disclaimer

## This is not professional advice

The agents in this repository produce **preparatory and advisory work product**. They are not a
substitute for a licensed Philippine professional, and nothing they produce is legal, tax,
accounting, financial, medical, engineering or regulatory advice.

In Philippine practice, specific work must be done or signed by a licensed professional:

| Work | Who must do or sign it |
| --- | --- |
| Audited financial statements | An independent CPA |
| Tax returns, as the signing preparer | A CPA or the taxpayer |
| Representation before the BIR, NLRC, DTI, SEC or a court | A lawyer, or an authorised representative |
| Contracts, articles of incorporation, by-laws | Reviewed by Philippine counsel |
| Notarisation | A notary public |
| Engineering plans and structural computations | A licensed engineer or architect |
| Construction contracting | A PCAB-licensed contractor with a sustaining technical employee |
| Customs declarations | A licensed customs broker |
| Safety certifications | An accredited safety practitioner |
| Real estate brokerage | A PRC-licensed broker |
| FDA product applications | Often a licensed pharmacist, chemist or food technologist |

Each agent names this in its own `## Limits` section and is instructed to say it to the user
rather than leave it implicit.

## Figures in these agents are last-known, not current

Philippine rates, thresholds, deadlines and wage orders change — sometimes mid-year, sometimes
under court injunction. Statutory contribution rates follow legislated schedules. Tax regulations
supersede each other. Agency requirements are revised by circular.

Every agent carries a mandatory `## Verify-before-advising` section naming the figures it must
re-check and the primary source for each, and is instructed to treat every peso amount in its own
prompt as last-known rather than current, and to state an as-of date whenever it quotes a figure.

**Verify before you act.** The primary sources are listed in
[`docs/PRIMARY_SOURCES.md`](docs/PRIMARY_SOURCES.md). Third-party articles — including
"2026 contribution table" pages — are frequently wrong, stale, or copied from each other.

## AI output can be wrong

These agents run on a language model. A model can state a superseded rule fluently, invent a form
number, or miscompute. The agents are designed to reduce that — by naming sources, by requiring
verification, and by showing their arithmetic — but they do not eliminate it.

Treat the output as a well-structured draft from a knowledgeable colleague who has not checked
today's circulars. Check anything that carries consequence.
